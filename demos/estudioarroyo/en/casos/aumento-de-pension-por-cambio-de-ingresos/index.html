Support increase after a change in income

Familia

I want something like this Ask on WhatsApp

The situation

Support had been set six years earlier, when the father was a salaried employee. Since then he'd started a company and his income had grown, but the support amount stayed the same.

What we did

We requested records from the tax and social security authorities to establish the real growth in income, and filed the request for an increase along with a breakdown of the children's current expenses.

The result

The court set support three times higher, with automatic adjustment by price index.
