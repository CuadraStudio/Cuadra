How child support is calculated in Uruguay

There's no fixed percentage, even though everyone repeats one. Here's what the court actually looks at.

I often hear that support is 30% of your salary. That number doesn't exist in the law.

What the court looks at

  • The child's actual needs: school, health, housing, activities
  • The paying party's real financial capacity, not just their pay stub
  • Time spent with each parent

Why the pay stub isn't enough

When there's income that doesn't show up on a pay stub, you can request records from the tax and social security authorities. It's the most effective route for self-employed workers.

Support can be changed

If needs or income change, an increase or reduction can be requested. It doesn't stay fixed for life.
