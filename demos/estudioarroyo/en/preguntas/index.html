FAQ

What people ask us most, answered.

How much does a consultation cost?

The first guidance response by WhatsApp or email is free. The full 45-minute consultation has a fee we'll tell you when you book, and it's deducted from the total if we go on to work together.

How are fees charged?

On labor claims, as a percentage of what's actually collected: if you don't collect, there's no fee. On family matters, a fixed amount agreed at the start. Always in writing and before we begin.

How long does a labor trial take?

Between one and two years at first instance. That's why, when there's a reasonable offer, it's almost always worth settling: you get paid much sooner.

Is there a deadline to claim a layoff?

One year from termination for labor claims, with exceptions. If more time has passed, ask anyway — some situations toll the deadline.

Do you take video calls?

Yes. The initial consultation and follow-up can be done by video call, and documents are signed digitally when the law allows it.

Do you work outside Montevideo?

Yes, by video call, and for hearings outside Montevideo we coordinate with correspondent colleagues.

What documents should I bring to the first consultation?

For labor matters: pay stubs from the last two years, any termination notices, and the severance settlement. For family matters: ID documents, children's birth certificates, and any prior agreement, if there is one.

Do you have agreements with any union or health plan?

We work with two unions and a co-op. If you're a member, let us know when you reach out, since it changes the fee.

Is the consultation confidential?

Yes, absolutely. Attorney-client privilege covers everything you tell us, even if we don't end up working together.

Still have a question?

Message us on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you.
