How we work

No fine print: here's the path from when you write to us until the matter is closed.

The question we get most is how much it costs. The honest answer is that it depends on the case, but the criteria aren't a mystery, and we explain them below.

The process, step by step

  1. 1

    You write to us

    By WhatsApp, email, or the form. Tell us the situation in a couple lines; you don't need to know legal terms.

  2. 2

    First guidance consultation

    Forty-five minutes, in person or by video call. I'll tell you if there's something to claim, what deadlines apply, and what documents you'll need. If there's nothing to be done, I'll tell you that too.

  3. 3

    Written estimate

    If there's a case, I'll send a written estimate, with the fee arrangement and case expenses listed separately. Nothing starts without your signature.

  4. 4

    We work the case

    I'll keep you posted on every development. You won't have to chase me to find out how it's going.

  5. 5

    Closing

    When it closes, I'll hand over the full digitized file and an accounting of expenses.

How fees work

We work with three fee arrangements depending on the type of matter. The one that applies is agreed in writing before we start.

  • Labor claims: percentage of what's actually collected. If you don't collect, there's no fee.
  • Divorce and family matters: a fixed amount agreed at the start, in installments if needed.
  • One-off consultations and filings: a per-item fee, per the Bar Association's fee schedule.

What to expect from us

  • A response within 24 business hours
  • Every filing explained before it's submitted
  • A written estimate, with no costs that show up later
  • If the case isn't worth taking to court, we'll tell you

FAQ

Is the first consultation free?

The first guidance response by WhatsApp or email is free. The full 45-minute consultation isn't, and its cost is deducted from the fee if we go on to work together.

Do you take clients outside of Montevideo?

Yes, by video call. For hearings outside the capital we coordinate with correspondent colleagues.
