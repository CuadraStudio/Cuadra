Referral program

If you refer us, we'll credit it toward your next consultation.

No card, no points that expire: everyone who reaches out because you sent them gets noted under your name.

How it works

  1. 1

    Refer the firm

    To a coworker, a family member, anyone. Have them mention your name when they write to us.

  2. 2

    Earn a consultation

    For every referral who becomes a client, you earn a credit toward your next consultation.

  3. 3

    Redeem whenever you want

    From two referrals on, your next one-off consultation is free.

  • No limit on referrals
  • Credits don't expire
  • Also applies to follow-up packs
