The brand

Vera Store, since 2021

Vera Store started as a table at a Pocitos market. Camila sold earrings she made herself and some pieces from workshop friends.

Today we're a small shop on 21 de Setiembre and we still follow the same idea: a few well-chosen pieces, from people we know.
