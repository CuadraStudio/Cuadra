Shipping & pickup

Pick up in Pocitos at no cost, or we'll deliver. Everything is coordinated on WhatsApp.

Once your order comes in, we message you to confirm availability and coordinate. The purchase is only finalized then: nothing is charged through the website.

Orders placed before 15:00 ship the same day, Monday to Friday.

FAQ

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes, in Montevideo you can pay cash on delivery. For the interior we ask for a bank transfer beforehand, since the courier is paid at drop-off.

Do you ship the same day?

Yes, in Pocitos and nearby, if the order comes in before 15:00. We'll confirm on WhatsApp.
