The studio

A barbershop that works like a studio

Estudio Trazo opened in 2019 in an old house in Parque Rodó. The idea was simple: cuts that hold up, in a place where nobody rushes you.

We don't work by volume. Every slot gets the time it needs, which is why we ask for a booking: nobody waits standing at the door, and everyone gets the same treatment.
