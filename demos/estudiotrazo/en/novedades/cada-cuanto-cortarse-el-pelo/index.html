How often you should get a haircut (based on your cut)

There's no single rule: it depends on length, texture, and how much you're willing to fight it every morning.

The question we get asked most at the studio is how often to come back. The short answer is three to six weeks, but the useful one depends on the cut you have.

Short cuts with a fade

A fade looks grown out at two and a half weeks. If a tidy look matters to you, three weeks is the max.

Medium and long cuts

Here you can stretch it to six or eight weeks. What breaks down first isn't the length but the ends and the neckline.

How to tell

  • Styling takes longer than usual
  • Hair parts where it used to fall on its own
  • The neckline looks messy from the side

If any of the three is happening, it's time.
