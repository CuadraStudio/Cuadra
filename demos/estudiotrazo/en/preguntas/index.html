Support

FAQ

What people ask us most, answered.

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

You can walk in, but we'll fit you in based on availability. With a booked slot you don't wait: you're seen at the time you booked.

How do I cancel or change my slot?

Through the link in your confirmation. You can reschedule or cancel yourself, no call needed. Please give us at least 2 hours' notice.

What happens if I'm late?

We hold your slot for 10 minutes. After that, if there's another booking behind you, we'll reschedule you so we don't run late for everyone else.

How can I pay?

Cash, debit, credit and bank transfer. We never charge anything from the website.

Do you take kids?

Yes, up to age 12 with the kids' haircut service. First cuts come with a certificate.

Is there parking?

There's metered parking on Bulevar. In Cordón, there's usually free parking on Colonia.

Do you sell products?

Yes, we carry the pomades, oils and shampoo we use. Available in store.

Can I book with a specific barber?

Yes. When booking you choose who you want, or let the system give you the first one available.

Still have a question?

Message us on WhatsApp and we'll get back to you.
