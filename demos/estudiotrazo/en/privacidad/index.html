Privacy policy

This site is a demo

Estudio Trazo, its details, credentials and all the cases, reviews, prices and contact information are fictitious and do not correspond to any real person or business. In this demo, simulated submissions are stored only in the visitor's browser (localStorage) and are never sent to any server. This text describes how information would be handled if the site were live for a real business.

What data we ask for

Only what we need to help you: name, phone and, if you leave it, your email. Estudio Trazo never asks for card details or ID documents: there's no online payment on this site.

What we use it for

To confirm your request, remind you of your booking or order, and reply if you write to us. Nothing else.

Who we share it with

No one. We don't sell or hand over data to third parties. Messages travel over WhatsApp and email, subject to those services' own policies.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed to assist you, plus any legal record-keeping requirement. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by writing to hola@estudiotrazo.uy. We respond within the timeframes set by Uruguay's Law 18.331 on Personal Data Protection.

Cookies

This site uses local browser storage to remember preferences and, where relevant, your in-progress order. We don't use advertising cookies.
