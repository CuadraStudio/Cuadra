FAQ

How many people fit in the room?

Up to 40 seated. For more, you can book the whole venue on Sunday nights.

Can we bring our own cake?

Yes, no cake corkage fee. Just let us know so we can keep it chilled.

Can we hire music?

Yes, until 1am per local regulations. We have sound equipment available.
