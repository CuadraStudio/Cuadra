The house

Twenty years tending the same fire

El Fogatero opened in 2005 on a Cordón side street where there was nothing. Nélida set up the grill and her son Ramiro took charge of the kitchen.

We still buy meat from the same supplier as always and produce from the Tristán Narvaja market on Saturdays. The menu changes little, and on purpose: we'd rather do five things well than twenty so-so.
